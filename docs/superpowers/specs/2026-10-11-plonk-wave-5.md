# Plonk: fifth wave (aligning with WorldGuessr as it actually plays)

Date: 2026-10-11. Builds on the earlier specs; where they differ, this one wins.

## Why

An independent audit compared Plonk row by row with WorldGuessr's code (not just its docs, which are out of date). Plonk has the same menu of modes but departs on many rules and lacks a long tail of features. This wave aligns the rules with WorldGuessr's code and builds the missing features a player would notice. Each item is marked **S** (server), **B** (browser) or **S+B**. The exact request and reply shapes for S+B items are in `2026-10-11-plonk-wave-5-api.md`, written by the server builder before either side implements them.

## A. Rules to align

1. **Scoring (S+B).** World scale 20,000 km. A guess within 30 m is perfect, and a computed score above 4,997 becomes 5,000. Region, country and community maps keep the diagonal of their places as the scale.
2. **Hint (B).** Radius `5870 km × scale / 20000 × cos(latitude of the place)`. At most 2 hints per singleplayer game. No hint in the daily, in matches or in streak.
3. **Singleplayer options (B).** Rounds stay 3, 5 or 10 (default 5). Timer becomes off, or 10 to 300 seconds in steps of 10. A "Road names" switch, off by default (Neel asked for none; WorldGuessr shows them).
4. **Daily (S+B).** 3 rounds, 60 seconds, moving, maximum 15,000. One attempt per day: after it, the daily cannot be played again. The three places are in three different countries on at least two continents. The day is UTC (WorldGuessr uses local midnight; a single shared day is simpler and fair).
5. **Public unranked (S).** 45 second rounds, up to 10 players. Join under way needs at least 3 rounds left, fewer than 10 players, and more than 15 seconds left in the current round.
6. **Pacing (S+B).** 5 second get-ready before each round. Reveal lasts 10 seconds (8 in a duel, 5 after the last round). When everyone but one player has guessed, the timer drops to 20 seconds if more remain; in a duel, when the opponent has guessed it drops to 15.
7. **Ranked duel (S+B).** At most 5 rounds, moving allowed, reactions only (no free chat). Leaving before anyone has guessed in round 1 cancels the match with no rating change and puts the other player back in the queue. A second such dodge within an hour locks that player out of the ranked queue for 120 seconds. Ranked games earn double XP.
8. **Rating (S).** New accounts start at 500; floor 100. Expected score on a 340 scale. K is 40 for a player's first 30 rated games, 20 up to 100, then 10; capped at 20 from rating 900 and 10 from 1,000. Each player's change uses their own K, with a minimum change of 1. Wins over the same opponent on the same day pay progressively less and nothing from the seventh.
9. **Leagues (S+B).** Trekker from 0, Explorer 800, Voyager 1,000, Nomad 1,300, Legend 1,800.
10. **Matchmaking (S).** Ranked pairs by rating: a window of ±50 that widens with time waited, closest rating first, and not the same opponent as the player's last ranked match within 30 minutes while anyone else is waiting.
11. **2v2 (S+B).** Each team shares 5,000 health. A team's round score is its best member's. The lower team loses the difference × 1.3. 5 rounds, 60 seconds. Players can queue alone or as a pair (two friends in a two-player party). Teammates see each other's pin live during the round. Chat has a Team/All switch.
12. **Chat (S+B).** Free chat only in parties and 2v2. Public unranked and ranked have reactions only. Twenty reactions, all free.

## B. Features to add

13. **Guests online (S+B).** A visitor can play public unranked and join parties as "Guest #NNNN" without signing up. Guests have no rating, XP, friends, maps or chat, and cannot play ranked or 2v2.
14. **Play again (S+B).** The final screen of a queued match has "Play again" (re-queue). A party's host has "Back to lobby", which returns everyone still there to the lobby with the same code.
15. **Party options (S+B).** Capacity 20 (WorldGuessr allows 200; polling makes that unaffordable here). Rounds 1 to 20. Time 10 to 300 seconds or no timer (a round then ends when everyone has guessed, or the host ends it). Communication: chat, reactions only, or off. Team mode: two teams, scored by best guess or by average, teams shuffled by the host or picked by players. Host controls: kick, hand over host, lock the party, end the round. Joining a party under way from the next round.
16. **Country Guesser and Continent Guesser (S+B).** Two solo modes: 10 rounds, six buttons (a flag and name, or a continent), 1,000 points per correct answer, 20 XP each, best score kept on the account.
17. **Daily depth (S+B).** A streak with one grace day per seven; a home indicator (not played, streak at risk, done); countdown to the next daily; leaderboard top 100, paged, with the player's own row when outside it; a score distribution with "beat X% of players"; a text share (score, percentage beaten, one coloured square per round, link); personal records and a 14-day history strip; a visitor's results kept in the browser and claimed by their account when they sign up.
18. **Result polish (B).** Country flag beside the country name; a "Perfect" mark at 5,000 and a short confetti burst from 4,850 (respecting reduced motion); the final screen shows one to three stars by percentage and counts the score up; the round table adds time taken and XP; "Share result" copies a text summary.
19. **Round screen (B).** Timer turns yellow under 10 seconds and red and pulsing under 5. The pin map gains fullscreen and keep-open buttons alongside the three sizes, and a map type choice (streets, satellite, terrain) from free tile sources with their attribution. Keys: A and D turn, plus and minus zoom. Clicking the compass faces north. A 5 second versus intro before a ranked duel.
20. **Maps (S+B).** A Liked list. Popular sorted by likes. Search over name, description and maker, from 3 characters. The map page previews its places in Street View, cycling five of them. Places can also be added by pasting Street View links or coordinate lists. Limits: 5 to 5,000 places, name 3 to 30, a short description (20 to 100, required) and a long one (up to 1,000).
21. **Accounts (S+B).** Change password; delete account with a 30-day restore window; a country on the account (guessed from the browser's locale, changeable), shown as a flag beside names everywhere; a login streak; duel wins, losses and ties with win rate and global rank; usernames may not start, end or double an underscore and go through the word mask. Sign-in stays username and password: Google, Apple and email-code sign-in need credentials for outside services that this project does not have.
22. **History (S+B).** Finished online games (duels, 2v2, unranked, parties) are saved per player. The history list is paged and covers every mode.
23. **Profile and boards (S+B).** Charts for XP, XP rank, rating and rating rank over 7 days, 30 days or all time. Profiles have a view counter and an add-friend button. Leaderboards show the top 100, flags, medals for the top three, a past-day view of each board, and leave out players inactive for 14 days.
24. **Friends (S+B).** Online or last-seen status; an "allow friend requests" switch.
25. **Chat controls (S+B).** Mute a player for the rest of a match; a setting to hide chat.
26. **Settings (B).** Units default to miles for US and UK browsers. A volume slider for sound effects.
27. **Presence (S+B).** A count of players online on the home screen. The queue screen shows elapsed time and, for ranked, the current rating window.
28. **Repeat avoidance (B).** The browser remembers the last 5,000 places it has shown and prefers unseen ones when drawing a game that is not a challenge link, a daily or a match.
29. **About (B).** An About pane: what Plonk is, how scoring works, FAQ, credits for imagery and maps, and a short changelog.

## Out of scope, and why

- **Ads, shop and "Stamps" cosmetics:** monetisation, not play.
- **Google, Apple and email-code sign-in:** need accounts with outside providers.
- **Native iOS and Android apps:** the installable web app from wave 4 stands in.
- **Placement match against a bot, harder place pool for high ratings, rating refunds after a cheater is banned, forced renames, temporary bans with appeals, season badges and Hall of Fame:** operations for a large live service; nothing a player on a small site would meet.
- **Learn mode** (switched off on WorldGuessr itself), **ChinaGuessr** (Baidu imagery), **background music.**
- **Party capacity of 200:** see item 15.
